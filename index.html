What the photos show

    Evergreen heath layer (photo-calibrated)

    Mountain laurel and great rhododendron cover, fitted to photo tags through each photo's footprint (where it could be standing and what it could see), with 2022 leaf-off lidar, leaf-off color-infrared and seasonal imagery as predictors. The March lidar sees these evergreen shrubs in full leaf, so lidar understory density and the 0.5–5 m layers carry the most weight, along with distance from developed land and wetland edges.
